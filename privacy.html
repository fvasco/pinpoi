Privacy Policy for PinPoi

Last Updated: January 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how PinPoi ("we," "our," or "the App") handles the collection, use, and disclosure of user information. Your privacy is a priority, and we are committed to protecting any data processed during your use of the App in compliance with Google Play Store policies.

1. Data Collection and Usage

PinPoi is designed to operate with minimal data requirements to ensure user privacy.

  • Personal Information: The App does not collect, store, or share any personally identifiable information (such as name, email address, or phone number) on its own servers.
  • Location Data: If you grant permission to use localization services, this data is used strictly in real-time to populate input fields and enhance your search for points of interest. This data is processed locally on your device and is not transmitted to us or stored permanently.

2. Third-Party Services

To provide specific features and support the App, we use third-party services that may process data according to their own privacy policies:

Advertising (Google AdMob)

We use Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect and use data such as your device's Advertising ID, cookies, and usage data to personalize ads or measure their performance.

  • Google AdMob Privacy Policy

Geocoding (Android Geocoder)

When you search for an address, the App utilizes the Android Geocoder service installed on your smartphone. Data processing for this service is governed by Google’s Terms of Service.

Maps (OpenStreetMap)

Map visualization is provided by OpenStreetMap, as displayed on the screen. These services may process technical data (such as IP addresses) to deliver map tiles.

  • OpenStreetMap Privacy Policy

3. Data Retention and Deletion

Since PinPoi does not collect or store personal data on its own servers, there is no personal data stored by the developer to be deleted. Any data processed by third-party services (like AdMob) is managed according to their respective retention policies.

4. Security

We prioritize the security of your information. While no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, the App is built to minimize data exposure by relying on local processing and established third-party providers.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. Changes are effective immediately after they are posted on this page.
